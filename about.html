About Kumaraguru College of Technology

Building Knowledge, Character and Innovation

Who We Are

Kumaraguru College of Technology (KCT), Coimbatore, is one of Tamil Nadu's leading autonomous engineering institutions.

The institution focuses on academic excellence, innovation, entrepreneurship, research and industry collaboration. Students are encouraged to develop technical knowledge, leadership skills and professional ethics through project-based learning.

KCT offers an excellent learning environment, modern laboratories, smart classrooms, research centres, innovation hubs and active student clubs.

Vision

To become a globally recognised institution by creating socially responsible engineers through quality education, innovation and research.

Mission

To provide world-class education, promote innovation, encourage entrepreneurship and prepare graduates for successful global careers.
